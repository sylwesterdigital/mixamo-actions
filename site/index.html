/ CAPABILITIES

Everything in one workflow.

01 /

Exact motion selection

Collect animation cards by their motion ID and full metadata. Keep named presets and reuse them across character exports.

02 /

Browser-assisted automation

Reuse your manually authenticated Brave session. Playwright handles animation selection, export preferences and completed downloads.

03 /

Single combined GLB

Blender assembles a character with textured materials, rig, and individually named animation tracks into one asset.

04 /

Interactive studio preview

Inspect your model in Three.js, switch clips, control playback, speed, transitions and lighting without leaving the studio.

05 /

Versioned local updates

Drop signed-by-digest versioned release archives into a watched folder. Tests, installation, restart and recovery are automated.

06 /

Source-controlled delivery

Connect your private Rantlist Stage gallery to upload animated GLBs, preview remotely stored avatars, and manage models from the Studio. Publish the static project homepage independently.

/ INSTALLATION

Designed for your Mac.

Local Flask Studio stays on your machine. The update watcher observes a versioned updates/ directory, manages the working core/ environment, and optionally publishes source changes and this homepage.

cd /Users/smielniczuk/Documents/works/mixamo-actions
./mixamo-studio-watch.sh
# Copy mixamo-actions-vX.Y.Z.zip into updates/
Requires an authenticated Brave session for Mixamo automation and an installed Blender executable. This public website is documentation—not a hosted Mixamo service.
